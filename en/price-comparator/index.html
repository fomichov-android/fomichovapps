How sneaky marketing makes you overpay

Manufacturers rarely raise prices outright — they quietly downsize the package weight or volume instead.

✕ Sneaky retail traps at the shelf:
  • • Misleading package sizes: an 870 ml bottle priced slightly lower looks cheaper than a 1000 ml bottle, even though its per-liter cost is significantly higher.
  • • The bulk-buy illusion: “Family packs” or promo bundles often cost more per unit than standard single packages.
  • • Tedious calculations: dividing prices by odd weights like 730 grams in a standard calculator app is slow, clumsy, and error-prone.
✓ How the app solves it:
  • • Common denominator: instantly converts any weight, volume, or count into a true unit price per 1 kg, 1 liter, or single item.
  • • Instant verdict: the best value option is immediately highlighted in green, while other products display the exact % you overpay.
  • • History & price updates: save calculations tagged by store and edit prices or weights in saved records at any time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why not just use the phone's built-in calculator?

A standard calculator forces you to divide prices by odd numbers of grams, multiply by 1,000, memorize intermediate figures, and manually calculate percentage differences. In Price Comparator, the keypad is ready on launch: enter the price and quantity in standard grams or milliliters, and the app converts everything to cost per 1 kg or 1 liter and clearly highlights the winner.

Does it require mobile internet at the store?

No. The app does not download remote databases and operates 100% offline. You can comfortably compare prices in basement grocery levels or metal-clad wholesale clubs without any mobile signal.

How does the app help remember prices across different supermarkets?

Every calculation can be saved with the store name and a short description. You can instantly locate past records via search, sort them, and if a store price changes, edit the existing entry directly without having to create a new one from scratch.
