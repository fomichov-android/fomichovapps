Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't the app automatically link with banks?

Bank syncing requires sharing sensitive account credentials or bank APIs with third-party aggregators, creating inherent security vulnerabilities. Furthermore, automatic tracking detaches people from actual awareness of their spending. Manual 5-second logging builds mindfulness without risking your banking credentials.

Does Expense Sheet require an internet connection?

No. Expense Sheet is built with a strict Local-First architecture. All entries stay inside an on-device database, allowing seamless tracking while traveling or in areas without cell reception.

How do I migrate records to a new phone?

The settings screen features Backup and CSV Export functions. You can export a full database backup (.db) to your phone's storage and tap “Restore database” on your new device, or export a CSV spreadsheet for Excel analysis.

Is there a subscription fee?

No subscriptions. The core expense tracker is free to use. An optional one-time lifetime purchase (ExpenseSheet Premium) removes ads and unlocks unlimited accounts, categories, and backup features forever.
