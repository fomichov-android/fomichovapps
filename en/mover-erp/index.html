✓ With the MoverERP app:

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it require internet connectivity on the job?

No. The app was built for autonomous offline field work. You can calculate jobs in basements, concrete towers, or on highways with zero cellular signal. Internet is only used when sending breakdowns to customers via messaging apps.

Can customers see my internal expenses or crew payouts?

Never. The client receipt is generated separately: it only displays the truck rate, worked hours, stairs, mileage, and final amount. Your cash registers, profits, and dispatcher percentages remain strictly confidential on your phone.

What happens after the 20 free jobs are completed?

All previously saved jobs, client contacts, and rates remain viewable. To add new jobs, the app will prompt you to activate the affordable PRO monthly subscription through Google Play.

How do I migrate my database to a new phone?

The settings screen features a Backup function. Export your backup file to device storage or send it to yourself, then tap “Restore from file” on your new device.
