Why Traditional Dictionary Memorization Fails

A single verb can carry dozens of shades of meaning depending on the particle and context. Without ear training and live sentence practice, your brain mixes them up.

✕ Dictionary list rote learning:
  • • Particle confusion: it is easy to mix up give up, give in, and give away when stripped of real sentences.
  • • Listening barrier: you might recognize a verb in writing, but miss it completely when spoken at natural speed.
  • • Repeating the same mistakes: tricky verbs get buried in giant lists and are never practiced enough to stick.
✓ The Get It! Multi-Mode Approach:
  • • 3 Game Modes (Text, Audio, Context): match verb cards with translations, recognize verbs purely by ear (TTS), or fit them into real sentences with a missing verb.
  • • Smart FOCUS Mode: the app automatically gathers verbs you've missed into a dedicated "Growth Zone" until you master them.
  • • Gamified XP, Ranks & Streak: earn combo and speed bonuses, unlock new modes in the XP shop, and protect your streak with Streak Freezes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What English levels and languages are supported?

Get It! covers all CEFR levels from Elementary (A1, A2) and Intermediate (B1, B2) up to Advanced and Proficiency (C1, C2), plus a full MIX mode and the FOCUS mistake-recovery mode. Translations and UI are available in 10 languages (English, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Greek, and Hungarian).

Does it require continuous internet access?

No. All 470 phrasal verbs, definitions, example sentences, and game modes work 100% offline. Internet is only needed if you choose to back up and sync your progress to the cloud.

How do I manage my data or delete my account?

You can reset your local progress or permanently delete your cloud account in one tap directly inside the in-app Settings menu, or submit a request via our official Data Deletion page.
