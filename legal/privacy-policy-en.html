3. Application-Specific Data Practices

3.1. MoverERP: Moving & Freight (com.apps.fomichov.m.movererp)

#mover-erp
  • 100% On-Device Storage: All moving orders, vehicle hourly rates, client contact records, job notes, crew split calculations, dispatcher commissions, debtor lists, and cash/card register balances are stored exclusively in the local SQLite database on your smartphone. The developer operates no external servers to collect or view your business records.
  • Client Breakdowns & Backups: Generating client cost breakdowns (as text or image) and exporting database backups occur strictly upon your manual action using standard Android system share sheets or local storage pickers.
  • Zero Advertising Networks: MoverERP contains zero third-party advertising SDKs. It operates on a transparent Freemium model (20 full-featured orders free, followed by an optional PRO subscription processed securely via Google Play Billing).

3.2. Linear Cutting: Optimization (com.gmail.fomichov.m.linearcutting)

#linear-cutting

All cut lists, profile geometry settings, warehouse bar stock, usable remnants, and transaction logs are stored locally on your device. File storage access is used solely when you manually import cut lists from Excel (.xlsx, .csv) or export PDF cutting layouts, QR label sheets, Excel reports, or JSON backups to your phone.

3.3. Margin Pro: Profit Calculator (com.defun.checkmargins)

#margin-pro

All cost figures, markups, profit margins, tax rates, and saved commercial calculations remain strictly inside your device's local SQLite database and are never transmitted to remote servers. JSON backup export and import operate locally via the Android system file picker.

3.4. Expense Sheet: Budget Tracker (com.apps.fomichov.m.ExpenseSheet)

#expense-sheet

Expense Sheet never requests or accesses online banking credentials, bank APIs, or SMS messages. All accounts, balances, categories, and daily transactions are stored 100% locally on your device. Exporting ledgers to CSV or backing up the database (.db) is performed locally by the user.

3.5. Price Comparator & Calculator (com.gmail.fomichov.m.compareprice)

#price-comparator

All unit price comparisons (per 1 kg, 1 liter, or 1 piece), store names, and shopping notes are stored locally on your device. The app does not track store geolocation or upload your shopping history to external servers.

3.6. Get It! Phrasal Verbs (com.apps.fomichov.m.phrasalverbs)

#get-it

The 470-verb dictionary and card-matching modes run offline using the Android system Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine. To synchronize learning progress (mastered verbs, XP score, rank, daily streak, and FOCUS mistake list) across multiple devices, the app uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), storing only your anonymous/authenticated User ID (UID) and game progress metrics.

You can permanently delete your cloud account and all synced progress in one tap inside the app's Settings menu, or via our Data Deletion page.

3.7. Super Loto: Smart Generator (com.gmail.fomichov.m.superlotogenerator)

#super-loto

User-saved combinations and custom ticket notes are stored locally on your device. Internet access is used solely to fetch publicly available historical draw results (unl.ua) and verify Google Play purchases.

Mandatory Non-Gambling Disclaimer: Super Loto Generator is an independent statistical and analytical reference utility and is not affiliated with UNL. The app does not sell lottery tickets, does not accept bets, does not facilitate real-money gambling, and does not guarantee winnings.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA) & Data Deletion

  • Deleting Local App Data: Because local records reside on your smartphone, you can permanently erase them at any time via Android Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time under Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Deleting Cloud Data (Get It!): Step-by-step instructions for in-app and email-based cloud data deletion are available on our Data Deletion Policy page.
