User Account & Data Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety Policy • Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Offline Applications with Local On-Device Storage

The following applications operate autonomously and store all user records, calculations, and settings exclusively in your device's local internal memory:

  • MoverERP: Moving & Freight (com.apps.fomichov.m.movererp)
  • Linear Cutting: Optimization (com.gmail.fomichov.m.linearcutting)
  • Margin Pro: Profit Calculator (com.defun.checkmargins)
  • Expense Sheet: Budget Tracker (com.apps.fomichov.m.ExpenseSheet)
  • Price Comparator & Calculator (com.gmail.fomichov.m.compareprice)
  • Super Loto: Smart Generator (com.gmail.fomichov.m.superlotogenerator)

Because the developer does not collect or store these records on external servers, you can instantly and permanently erase all local data at any time:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps.
  2. Select the corresponding application.
  3. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage (Delete data), or simply uninstall the application from your device.

2. Cloud-Synced Application (Get It! Phrasal Verbs)

In Get It! Phrasal Verbs (com.apps.fomichov.m.phrasalverbs), optional cloud synchronization (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore) is used to sync learned vocabulary, XP, and daily streaks across devices.

You can permanently delete your cloud account and all associated learning data using either method below:

Method 1: Instant Deletion Inside the App

Open Get It! → go to Settings → Account → Delete Account & Data. All cloud and local records are permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Without Opening the App)

Send an email with the subject line Data Deletion Request: Get It! to fomichov.developer@gmail.com including your User ID (UID) or the Google account email used to sign in. All associated records will be permanently purged from Firebase servers within 7 business days.

3. Advertising Identifiers (AdMob / AppLovin)

You can reset or delete your device's Google Advertising ID at any time by navigating to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset / Delete advertising ID.
